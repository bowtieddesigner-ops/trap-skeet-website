Features

Built for the shooting line

Big, glanceable screens, button-first controls, and the stats you actually want after a round.

Field-map shot charts

After every round, see each station laid out the way the field looks, with a dot for every bird. Spot the station that cost you points at a glance, for trap, doubles, handicap, Olympic trap and skeet.

Hit and miss you can feel

A green glow for a hit, red for a lost bird, so you know the score without reading a thing. Station dots along the bottom show where you are in the round.

Personal bests and real stats

Get a banner the moment you beat your best streak or score. Lifetime stats track your accuracy, best and worst stations, and a chart of your last 10 rounds.

↶

Undo and resume

Fat-fingered a score? Undo it. Got called away mid-round? Pick up right where you left off.

◎

Doubles, all 50 targets

Full trap doubles support, scoring both birds of every pair across all 50 targets.

⤳

Wind arrow

Live wind speed and direction while you shoot, saved with each round alongside the weather.

♥

Garmin Connect laps

Rounds save as activities with a lap per station, so heart rate and timing line up with every post.
