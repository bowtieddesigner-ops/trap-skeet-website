FAQ

Questions shooters ask

What is Trap and Skeet?

Trap and Skeet is a scoring app for clay shooting that runs on Garmin watches. You record each target as a hit or a miss from your wrist, and the app keeps score, saves the round to Garmin Connect, and shows a field-map shot chart afterward.

How do I score trap on a Garmin watch?

Install Trap and Skeet from the Garmin Connect IQ Store, open it on your watch, and choose Trap, then Singles, Doubles, Handicap or Olympic. Pick your starting station. After each target, press the Up button for a hit or the Down button for a miss. On touchscreen watches you can also tap the left half of the screen for a hit or the right half for a miss.

Which game types does it support?

Five: trap singles (25 targets), trap doubles (all 50 targets), trap handicap with your yardage, Olympic trap, and skeet including the option bird.

Which Garmin watches are supported?

76 Garmin watch models, including fēnix, epix, Forerunner, Venu, vívoactive, Instinct, Enduro, MARQ, Descent, D2 and Approach watches. The Connect IQ Store listing shows whether your exact model is supported.

Can I undo a shot or pause a round?

Yes. Press Back during a round to open the pause menu, where you can undo the last shot, resume, or discard the round. If you leave the app mid-round, you can resume the unfinished round from the home screen.

Does it save rounds to Garmin Connect?

Yes. Each round is saved as an activity in Garmin Connect, with a separate lap for each station so heart rate and timing line up with every post.

What stats does it track?

Each round has a shot chart, summary, weather and location. Lifetime stats include overall accuracy, best and worst station, best and worst starting station, most-missed bird, average round time, average heart rate, a chart of your last 10 rounds, and alerts when you set a new personal best.

Is there an Apple Watch or Samsung Galaxy Watch version?

Not yet. Apple Watch and Samsung Galaxy Watch (Wear OS) versions are coming soon. Today the app is available on Garmin watches through the Connect IQ Store.
